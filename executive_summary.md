# Executive Summary: Bonsai

## STAR Overview

**Situation**: AI adoption among developers has reached 90%, yet distrust has surged by 15pp. Production-grade codebases are suffering from "AI pollution": unscoped diffs, architectural drift, and review bottlenecks that turn 5-minute tasks into 45-minute cleanup sessions.

**Task**: Establish a new standard for AI-assisted development that prioritizes predictability, control, and architectural alignment over raw generation speed. The goal is to move from "chat-and-hope" to "spec-and-verify."

**Action**: We are building **Bonsai**, an IDE-native toolset for JetBrains that implements a "Spec-First" workflow. 
- **Wedge**: Deep PSI integration for change containment (diff control).
- **Core**: Repos-native, markdown-based specifications that live inside the developer's current workflow.
- **Scale**: A governance layer for Engineering Managers to enforce standards across large-scale, multi-repo organizations.

**Result**: 
- **Efficiency**: 20-30% reduction in review churn.
- **Quality**: 100% alignment between implementation and architectural intent.
- **Strategy**: Position JetBrains as the industry standard for AI governance, creating a massive data moat through IDE-native integration.
